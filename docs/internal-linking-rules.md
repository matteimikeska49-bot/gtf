# Internal Linking Rules & Cluster Flow

This document defines the strict internal linking architecture for the GoToFlow SEO platform.

## Safety rules
* No published page may link to a draft/noindex/preview page.
* No internal link may point to a held/rejected topic.
* No broken internal links.

## Language rules
* EN content links only to EN content/product routes.
* RU content links only to RU content/product routes.
* Exceptions must be explicit and documented.

## Cluster rules
* **Supporting to Hub:** A supporting article must link to its hub article.
* **Hub to Supporting:** A hub article should link to key supporting articles when they are published.
* **Product Routing:** All articles must link to the correct cluster product route.
* **Explore limits:** Explore links must stay inside the language/cluster unless explicitly allowed.

## Draft rules
* D53 drafts may link to hub/product routes, but cannot be linked from published pages until published.
* If a hub is not published yet, a supporting article can have a planned link in the brief/cluster map but should not link to the unpublished hub from a public article body.

## Anchor text rules
* Anchor text should be natural and contextually relevant.
* Avoid generic anchors like "click here", "read more", "this post".
* Avoid over-optimized repetitive exact match anchors.
