# AI Carousel Workflow: From Idea to Ready-to-Publish Slides

Creating high-performing carousels for LinkedIn and Instagram usually involves a frustrating amount of friction. You have to write the text, count the characters, switch to design software, align text boxes, adjust font sizes, and export the files.

This manual process is the reason most creators are inconsistent. 

A modern AI carousel workflow changes the equation. It does not replace human creativity; it removes the mechanical friction of formatting. Here is the exact step-by-step workflow to go from a raw idea to a published carousel in under 30 minutes.

## Step 1: The Brain Dump (Human Task)

AI cannot invent your personal experience. The workflow starts with you. 

When you have an idea—a lesson learned, a client case study, or a framework—just get it down. Open a notes app, record a voice memo, or write a messy draft. Do not worry about spelling, grammar, or slide structure. 

The goal here is purely to capture the "meat" of your expertise.

## Step 2: Refinement and Structuring (AI Task)

Raw notes are hard to read. You need to structure them before they can become a carousel. 

If your notes are a mess, use an AI chat tool (like ChatGPT or Claude) to act as your editor. 

:::prompts
### The Structure Prompt
```text
Here are my raw notes on [Topic]. 
Please clean this up, remove the fluff, and organize the core 
insights into a logical list. Suggest a strong, curiosity-driven 
hook for the beginning.
```
:::

Review the output. Ensure the AI didn't remove any of your unique tone of voice or crucial context.

This is the phase that traditionally takes the longest: turning text into well-designed slides.

## Step 4: Human Review and Polish (Human Task)

Never publish AI-generated content blindly. You must act as the final art director and editor.

*   **Check the Hook:** Is the text on slide 1 massive and compelling? 
*   **Check the Flow:** Does each slide naturally lead into the next? 
*   **Check the CTA:** Is the final slide directing the user to take a specific action (e.g., "Join the newsletter" or "Leave a comment")?

If a slide feels too text-heavy, edit it down. 

## Step 5: Export and Publish

Different platforms require different file formats. 

*   **LinkedIn:** Export your carousel as a single PDF document. This triggers LinkedIn's document viewer, which is highly favored by the algorithm.
*   **Instagram:** Export your carousel as a series of high-resolution images (JPG or PNG) and upload them as a multi-image post.

The AI carousel workflow is a collaboration. You provide the unique insight, strategy, and final approval. The AI handles the editing, layout, and formatting.
