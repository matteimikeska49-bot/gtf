# How to Brainstorm Carousel Topics with AI

Staring at a blank screen is the most expensive part of content creation. You know your industry inside out, yet when it’s time to write a LinkedIn or Instagram carousel, your mind goes completely blank.

You end up writing a generic post, nobody saves it, and the cycle repeats. 

Using AI for brainstorming changes this dynamic. It shifts you from being an exhausted writer trying to invent ideas from scratch to an editor curating the best angles. 

## The Core Strategy: The Content Matrix

To never run out of ideas, you need a system. The Content Matrix is a proven method used by top creators, and AI is highly effective at populating it.

The matrix is simple: you cross **Core Pillars** (what you know) with **Proven Formats** (how people consume content).

:::cards
type: takeaways

### 1. Core Pillars
Identify 3-4 main topics you talk about. If you are a designer, these might be UI/UX, Typography, Freelancing, and Tools.

### 2. Proven Formats
Identify how information is best delivered. The most popular formats are: Step-by-Step, Common Mistakes, Myths, Case Studies, and Tools.

### 3. The Intersection
Combine them. "Typography" + "Mistakes" = *5 Typography Mistakes That Make Your UI Look Cheap*. 
:::

> [!tip]
> **Don't go too broad**
> A broad topic ("How to design a website") is boring. A specific micro-topic ("How to design a hero section that converts at 5%") gets saved and shared.

## Generating Topics with AI Prompts

If you just type "give me ideas for carousels" into an AI, you will get generic garbage. You must provide context: who you are, who you serve, and what format you want.

Use these proven prompt frameworks to generate high-quality topics.

:::prompts
### The Pain Point Extractor
```text
I am a [Your Profession]. My target audience is [Describe Audience]. 
List 10 specific, daily frustrations they face regarding [Your Niche]. 
For each frustration, suggest a "How-to" carousel topic that solves it.
```

### The Matrix Filler
```text
My core topic is [Topic]. Give me 5 carousel ideas based on these formats: 
1) A controversial opinion or myth, 2) A step-by-step framework, 
3) A list of common mistakes, 4) A "Before and After" case study, 
5) A list of recommended tools.
```

### The Hook Generator
```text
I want to write a carousel about [Specific Topic]. 
Generate 10 punchy, curiosity-driven hooks for the first slide. 
They should be under 10 words and make the reader want to swipe.
```
:::

## Common Brainstorming Pitfalls

Even with AI, you can generate the wrong type of content. Watch out for these traps:

:::cards
type: mistakes

### 1. Writing for your peers
If your audience is beginners, don't brainstorm complex, jargon-heavy topics that only your competitors will understand.

### 2. Lack of actionable advice
"The History of Marketing" is a bad carousel. "3 Marketing Tactics You Can Use Today" is a highly savable carousel.

### 3. Ignoring the CTA
Every topic must align with a business goal. If you brainstorm a topic but can't naturally tie it to a Call to Action on the final slide, discard it.
:::
